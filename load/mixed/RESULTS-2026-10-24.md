# The one-hour mixed run, 24 October 2026

One run of the mixed load on Ostia DevNet (chain 770101), from the log of the driver and read-only reads of the explorer's API. The figures are copied from the run's own report ([`results/2026-10-24/report.md`](results/2026-10-24/report.md), `report.json` and `summary.json` beside it) and from `/api/blocks`, `/api/canton/updates/<id>`, `/api/txs/<hash>` and `/api/stats/summary`, read afterwards. The run was not repeated.

## In short

- **When:** the driver sent from 12:59:28 to 13:59:58 UTC. The chain made blocks **224 to 448** (225 blocks, 12:59:29 to 13:59:43 UTC); every one of them holds only the load's transactions, and block 224 holds the first one (an approval).
- **What:** **13,496 transactions** accepted by the public RPC, from 200 wallets and the 4 verified ERC-3643 holders, in 25 kinds of action, deliberate reverts counted by case (the table below). **All 13,496 became final; none was lost.** The RPC refused 5 more at send (nonce gap, see below); they are not on the chain.
- **How fast:** 2, 3, 4 and 4.5 transactions a second for ten minutes each, then 4.5 for twenty minutes. Measured on the chain, by ten-minute window: 1.95, 2.98, 3.95, 4.58, 4.48 and 4.54 tx/s.
- **What limited the rate:** the public RPC allows 300 calls a minute to one client, and the driver kept to 285, which is about 4.5 sends a second once receipts are counted. The chain did not limit it (see "Blocks"): its fullest block had 84 transactions and 7.89 Mgas of 30 Mgas, and a block's proof took about 7.9 s at up to 30 transactions and about 9.1 s at 76 to 84 (the prover's busy ratio stayed at or under 0.56). **This run did not find the chain's limit.**
- **What did not work:** the RedStone vault recorded **no price** (RedStone's public gateways refuse anonymous requests); 4 transactions failed that should not have, and 57 deliberate reverts did not revert. All of it is explained below.

## Failures

The chain shows 292 failed transactions from the hour: 288 deliberate reverts that reverted as meant and 4 unexpected failures. The explorer's own 24 hour summary says 312 failed of 14,088 transactions; the other 20 failures and 592 transactions are from before the hour.

**Deliberate reverts, 288 of 345 reverted.** ERC-1155 bad id 62, unwrap more than held 61, ERC-721 not the owner 58, ERC-20 amount over balance 48, and the ERC-3643 transfer to an unverified address 59 (the explorer shows it as a revert with the reason "Transfer not possible", for example [`0x690e50a8…`](https://ostia.devnet.romeprotocol.xyz/tx/0x690e50a80a9a02cd00b638886f4a65cf01f8f8291b1eb5add21094009bf11a2c), block 338).

**The 57 that did not revert: a driver bug, fixed.** The case "swap: output below the minimum" built `amountOutMin` as 999,999 with 6 decimals, but the output token, tETH, has 18, so the minimum was 0.000000999999 tETH while 20 tUSD buys about 0.0066 tETH: every one of the 57 swaps went through. The driver now builds it with 18 decimals, and `./run.sh load check-reverts` checks all five deliberate cases with `eth_call` and sends nothing. Before the fix, on the chain: the swap case "DID NOT REVERT"; after it: `UniswapV2Router: INSUFFICIENT_OUTPUT_AMOUNT`, and the same call with a minimum of 0 goes through (the control that shows the revert is the right one, and not a missing allowance). The fix is not in the hour's figures: this run still has the 57.

**The 4 unexpected failures: one cause.** Three `swapTromeToUsd` swaps ([`0x82ae5f2c…`](https://ostia.devnet.romeprotocol.xyz/tx/0x82ae5f2c8e7327dc6fab778b8af68b66c66e578d27042b57bdf9567db280b23f), block 232; [`0x0ed2c4e5…`](https://ostia.devnet.romeprotocol.xyz/tx/0x0ed2c4e54897fed59c39e02270af265cb0f1dea245339d53d173a369f1b04583), block 234; [`0xd822c508…`](https://ostia.devnet.romeprotocol.xyz/tx/0xd822c5082fbafa7273e0590a091264eea50f4ecbdb252db3999fa4c9230ad87c), block 244) reverted with `TransferHelper::transferFrom: transferFrom failed`, and one unwrap of 0.2 ([`0xe8e5beb3…`](https://ostia.devnet.romeprotocol.xyz/tx/0xe8e5beb3dde7f5dcbdccf08ec057a32edd8dcf5205ad72310a33bcd3551c7673), block 235) with `ERC20: burn amount exceeds balance`. In each case the wallet held no WtROME: `balanceOf` read at the block before the failure is 0 for the wallet of the first swap, the second swap and the unwrap (read at block 231, before any of the three; nothing it did in between succeeded) and for the wallet of the third swap (read at block 243). The driver thought it held some because it books a wallet's WtROME when an action is planned, and the planned `swapUsdToTrome` (which would have paid 1 WtROME) had been **refused by the RPC at send** with a nonce gap; the wallet's next action, planned from that booking, spent WtROME it never got. Both wallets show the same order: a refused `swapUsdToTrome`, then the failing swaps (and the unwrap), then a `swapUsdToTrome` that went through later.

**The 5 refusals at send** (all "nonce gap: send the missing transactions first"; 2 `swapUsdToTrome`, 1 `swapTromeToUsd`, 1 `erc1155Mint`, 1 `erc20Transfer`): two transactions of one wallet were sent at once, waited for the RPC budget, and the later one reached the RPC first. Fixed after the run in two ways: one wallet's transactions now go to the RPC in nonce order, and what the driver booked for a transaction is taken back when the RPC refuses it or the transaction fails. The fix has not been run for an hour; `check-reverts` is the only part of it checked against the chain.

## RedStone: the vault recorded no price

`PriceVault` ([`0xe925b34b…`](https://ostia.devnet.romeprotocol.xyz/address/0xe925B34bCbaC24710353B3EF9331E88BF7329F6e)) is deployed and verified (block 97), and its only transaction is its creation. The driver asked RedStone's gateways for signed prices 80 times in the hour and got 0; it sent **0 `record` calls** (the planner reached the kind 689 times and could not build it), and 0 price reads answered. RedStone's public gateways reject anonymous requests (`public_gateway_rejected`, HTTP 403), in windows that grow each day until they close for good on 2026-10-29. To record prices, the oracle needs a data-service access arrangement with RedStone (a gateway and API key for the SDK's `authenticatedGateways`); this run had none, and none is part of the kit. Nothing in these results shows a RedStone price on the chain.

## Licences of what the kit installs

RedStone's packages (`@redstone-finance/evm-connector`, `-sdk`, `-protocol`) are **BUSL-1.1** throughout, the connector contracts and the JS `sdk` client alike, although each `package.json` says MIT; use is non-production only. TACEO's Merces contracts carry MIT in their SPDX headers (the repository has no `LICENSE` file). The full table is in the [README](README.md#what-is-installed-at-run-time-and-its-licence).

## What this run does not show

- The chain's limit: the RPC budget ended the climb first, and no block was larger than 84 transactions.
- Any block of more than 100 transactions.
- A working oracle leg, or a settled private transfer.
- Whether the driver's fixes hold for a full hour (only the deliberate-revert case was checked, with `eth_call`).
